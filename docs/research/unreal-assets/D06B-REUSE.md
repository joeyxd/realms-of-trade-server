## Slice and existing implementation

`src/data/raftparts.js` already defines `net` (makes 6 `pescado`, requires lona/madera) and `grill` (consumes 2 pescado, makes 2 `galleta`, capped at 6 batches/day). The fish item is `pescado` / “Pescado salado” in `src/data/goods.js`. `src/sim/economy/raft.js` already models the net and recipe work in `stepRaft`; activation, persistence safety, full-hold preflight, and visible stop reasons remain D06b implementation work described in the brief.

The editor list in `src/data/raftEditor.js` currently omits both pieces. The existing `src/render/rafts.js` constructs the net from four posts and a gridded mesh, and the grill from a wooden base, iron plate/grate, and fire-colored primitive. `src/render/raftMaterials.js` maps semantic wood, iron, rope, and cloth onto the already shipped shared comic atlas. It has a procedural-color fallback; assets do not gate either piece. The atlas is [desktop WebP](../../../assets/textures/raft/comic-materials-v1.webp) (308,536 B) and [mobile WebP](../../../assets/textures/raft/comic-materials-v1-mobile.webp) (82,878 B), selected as a single device-specific variant; details and limits are in `docs/delivery/raft-comic-material.md`.

## Recommendation

For the first chain, retain the existing hand-built net and grill and their atlas-backed wood/iron/cloth surfaces. Extend the editor only with `net` and `grill` as the implementation requires; no new asset export is a prerequisite. Keep the fish as the existing economy good unless product design specifically needs a visible fish prop/icon. Do not export `SM_RepairBench`, `SM_FishingBall`, bread assets, generic food UI, water/fire material, or eating audio for this chain. If later visual review finds a concrete gap, reopen just that exact candidate and verify conversion, style, dependency set, and mobile cost before integrating.

This is an asset reuse decision only; it does not authorize or accept D06b production, persistence, capacity, editor, or UI implementation.
